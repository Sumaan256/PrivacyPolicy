# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

Welcome, your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you play our game. Since our game is designed solely for fun and does not collect any personal data, there is very little to share. Please take a moment to read through the details below.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive information when you play the game. This means we do not ask for or store:
- Your name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Billing or payment information
- Any other personal details

The game is designed to be played without the need for any personal data.

## Usage Data

We do not track or store any gameplay data related to your performance, progression, or actions within the game. All gameplay is offline or anonymous, and no data is sent to our servers.

## Third-Party Services

We do not use third-party services for analytics, advertising, or other purposes that would require access to your data. The game is completely standalone with no external data collection.

## Data Retention

Since we do not collect any personal or gameplay data, there is nothing to retain. Once you close the game, no information is stored or remembered.

## Your Rights and Choices

As we do not collect or store any data, you do not need to worry about managing your personal information, opting out of communications, or deleting your data. Your privacy is fully respected.

## Security

Because no personal or sensitive information is collected or stored, there is no data to protect. However, we encourage all players to take precautions on their own devices to ensure their personal security while enjoying the game.

## Children’s Privacy

Our game is intended for all ages and does not collect any information from children under 13 or any other users. Since we do not gather personal data, there are no specific age-related restrictions on playing.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

Since we do not collect any personal data, this Privacy Policy is subject to very little change. If we update it in the future, we will make sure to reflect the new details in this document. Please check back periodically.
